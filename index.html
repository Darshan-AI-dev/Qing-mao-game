Five hundred years of memory. A mountain village. One chance to begin again.

Two hundred chapters of the Qing Mao arc: the rebirth, the awakening, the Liquor worm and the cave, the academy, the inheritance trials, the year-end duel, the winter crossing, the family assets, the wolf tide, the investigation, and the night the mountain came apart. Every chapter is played, staged, or recorded in his own ledger.

Before you begin

This story contains violence, cruelty, death and mass death, and many of the people involved are teenagers. Decisions and consequences happen on screen; violence is cut away from, and the aftermath is shown through places and people rather than detail.

Neither setting changes what happens in the story. You can change it at any time in Options.

Keyboard WASD to move · drag to look · E to interact · G to recall

Gamepad standard mapping, left stick to move, A to interact

On iPhone and iPad, Safari clears a site's saved data after seven days without a visit, which would lose a long save. Adding this game to your Home Screen exempts it. In Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open it from there.

A fan adaptation. All dialogue, inner-voice lines and ledger entries are original writing; nothing is transcribed from any translation. Progress is saved in this browser.

Short nods to arcs after chapter 200. Off by default; hints, never summaries.

Systems

A mortal Gu Master carries five or six at a time, as in the source. Anything past that waits in reserve — nothing is ever lost, and the Gu tab of the journal swaps them over.

Off by default: calling a Recollection up tells you how a fight goes, and working it out yourself earns the Foresight recognition. Turn this on to have the answer printed either way.

Canonical refinements can never fail outright either way — a bad attempt costs days and stones. Turning this off removes the roll from optional recipes too.

Story text

Archived sets hold every line, ledger entry and interface string as it read when the set was captured. Switching back is exact and nothing is lost.

Graphics

A recent phone can run High. The automatic choice is a starting point, not a ceiling.

Reading and motion

Also follows your system setting. Camera shake and most particle movement are removed.

Input

By default, dragging right turns the view right and dragging down looks down. The left stick moves Fang Yuan relative to the camera, so pushing up always walks away from you.

iOS Safari does not support vibration, so it is never the only signal for anything.
